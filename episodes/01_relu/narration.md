# Activation Function Series: ReLU

Approximately 38 seconds. Suggested narration only; the video is silent.

| Time | Narration |
| --- | --- |
| 0–4 s | ReLU means Rectified Linear Unit. Take the larger of zero and the input. |
| 4–14 s | Apply the rule to each entry. Minus two becomes zero. One stays one. Minus a half becomes zero. Three stays three. Minus four becomes zero. |
| 14–22 s | The same pairs become points on the graph. Negative inputs give zero. Positive inputs stay unchanged. |
| 22–31 s | ReLU follows a simple rule. Negative activations are turned off. Positive activations pass through unchanged. |
| 31–34 s | This change of rule introduces nonlinearity. |
| 34–38 s | Suppress negatives. Pass positives. This simple rule gives the network nonlinear behavior. |

In the suppression scene, moving points start on the faint identity reference
at (x, x) and move vertically to (x, 0), retaining their input coordinate.
Positive points remain at (x, x). OFF/PASS includes zero in PASS; zero stays zero.
